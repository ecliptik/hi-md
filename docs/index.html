“Hi-MD” Established: The Music Player Has Evolved into a Versatile Listening App for the Self-Hosted Era

October 2026 — ecliptik today announced the development of “Hi-MD,” marking an evolution of the music player into a versatile listening app that also delivers synced lyrics and a native Mac app, while maintaining playback compatibility with current Subsonic and Navidrome servers.

With the introduction of “Hi-MD,” your Navidrome server is poised to meet the demands of a growing broadband era with a versatile player capable of playing a variety of content, such as music and lyrics, while maintaining high quality sound and resolution.

High capacity
Up to four minutes of music is buffered ahead of playback. Upcoming, recently played, pinned and favourite songs can be stored on the device for offline listening, and whole albums can be downloaded.
High quality sound
Original-quality streaming on Wi-Fi, gapless playback and crossfades on a two-deck engine, a 10-band equalizer with AutoEq headphone corrections, and loudness leveling.
Versatile recording media
Native apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac. On the Mac, Miniamp is a Winamp-style player that loads classic skins (.wsz). Leverage Apple Intelligence, Anthropic or OpenAI to provide recommendations based on your unique taste.
Copyright protection technology
No OpenMG and no MagicGate. Hi-MD has no accounts and doesn’t track you.

ecliptik will proactively promote the “Hi-MD” format household-wide.

Now Hi-MD turns your iPhone, your Mac, and your car into a concert hall.

Press play and two decks go to work. Songs meet without a gap, or crossfade into each other, and stream in their original quality on Wi-Fi* with a lighter format for each kind of network. A 10-band equalizer takes AutoEq corrections for your headphones, and loudness leveling keeps one album from shouting over the next.

Lyrics scroll in time, karaoke-style. Discover deals out artist stations, Smart Shuffle, moods and your own Wrapped. Internet radio is a tap away. Album recommendations come from Apple Intelligence on the device, or from Anthropic or OpenAI with your own key,† checked against MusicBrainz and your library.

On the Mac it is a native app, with a Music-style library, a full-window Now Playing, a menu bar player and a MiniPlayer that shrinks down to a bar of controls. In the car, CarPlay.‡

The one and only sound of Hi-MD.

You could carry a lot more tunes.

Hi-MD buffers as though the network is out to get it. Songs buffer four minutes ahead on both decks, and the one that’s playing downloads in full from its first second, so a dead spot on the train is only a dead spot on the train.

Then it keeps things, for offline listening:

  • Upcoming songs
  • Recently played
  • Pinned songs
  • Favourites
  • Whole albums, for the commute

How it all works is written up in Playback Continuity.

It really whips.

Miniamp.

Play your music like it’s 2001. Miniamp is a compact player on the Mac that loads classic Winamp 2 skins, the .wsz files still sitting on a backup drive somewhere.

Bring out your old Winamp Skins and show off your style. Miniamp comes in its own custom skin, Hi-MD Gold, and zooms to 2× or 4× for modern hi-fi screens.

One library, on your own server.

Small print

Hi-MD

  1. * Original quality means the file as it sits on your server, so lossless files play lossless. On other networks Hi-MD picks a lighter format.
  2. † Anthropic and OpenAI recommendations use your own key. The names of recent and favourite albums, top artists and playlists are sent; the app lists them and asks before the first request. Server credentials are never sent. Privacy
  3. ‡ A CarPlay build for a device needs Apple’s CarPlay audio entitlement.

Hi-MD does not track you and has no accounts. Library and playback traffic goes directly to your server, and credentials are kept only in the Keychain.

Hi-MD is a fork of Minidisc by Loriage, itself a fork of Cassette by Mathieu Dubart. It talks to servers through SwiftSonic by Mathieu Dubart, and its headphone corrections come from AutoEq by Jaakko Pasanen.
